ABOUT US


The Travel Recommendation App helps users explore popular travel destinations through an interactive and user-friendly interface. it allows users to search for beaches, temples countries or specific country names to vire related cities along with images and descriptions. The App dynamically fetches data from a structured JSON file to display relevant results. it provides a simple and efficient way to discover and plan travel destinations.
